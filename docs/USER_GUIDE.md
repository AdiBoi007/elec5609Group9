# Circle Health User Guide

## Start here

Sign in with the demo account, then use **Dashboard** to answer three questions: how you are doing today, what has changed, and what to do next. Totals, the daily score, Today timeline, health calendar and progress views all read from the same persisted records.

Press **Cmd/Ctrl+K** anywhere to search Circle Health, ask a health-data question or run a compact command such as `500ml water`. The top-bar **Quick Log** action opens rapid entry without disrupting the current page.

## Logging health data

Open **Log** for the complete entry workspace:

- **Meal:** search recent or frequent foods, scan a barcode, create a custom food, adjust quantities and review running macro totals before saving.
- **Workout:** name the session, add exercises, sets, reps and load, then review the sticky session summary.
- **Water:** use quick quantities or enter a custom positive amount.
- **Sleep:** record bedtime, wake time, quality and optional notes. Sleep is assigned to the wake date.
- **Body:** record any applicable measurements; only the date and at least one measurement are required.

Successful writes remain on the Log page and update its Today summary. Detailed history, edit and delete controls remain on the relevant feature pages.

## Goals and progress

Open **Goals** to create body, nutrition, hydration, sleep, workout-frequency or streak goals. Circle Health uses existing health records as the source of truth; a goal never creates duplicate measurements. Body-goal projections appear only when enough dated trend data exists.

Open **Progress** to switch between week and month ranges, inspect the adherence calendar, compare this week with last week, review recovery and drill into a day. A day without a workout remains neutral unless a workout was logged.

## Ask Circle

Ask Circle answers questions such as “How is my protein this week?” or “Am I on track for my weight goal?” using saved profile and health data. It also provides remaining-macro meal ideas and a deterministic Finish My Day checklist. These are general fitness and wellness suggestions, not medical advice.

## Plans and groceries

Workout and meal plans can be generated and saved without an API key through deterministic fallbacks. Expand meal-plan days for full meal detail, then create a grocery list. Grocery checks, quantities and custom items persist after refresh.

## Appearance and data

Go to **Settings → Appearance** to choose System, Light or Dark. The choice is stored on the device. Use **Settings → Data** to export user-owned records, including goals, as CSV.
